Nina is the healer of [Far Horizon]({{ '/wiki/factions/other/far-horizon/' | relative_url }}), an active Rank B adventurer party.

## Appearance

Nina is a young woman with copper-red hair. She wears a maid-inspired traveling outfit in dark navy and white and carries a simple healer's staff set with a blue magic stone. Her clothing is unusual for an adventurer, but remains practical enough for field travel.
