Gustav serves as the defender and frontline combatant of [Far Horizon]({{ '/wiki/factions/other/far-horizon/' | relative_url }}), an active Rank B adventurer party.

## Appearance and Equipment

Gustav is a large adventurer who wears practical, battle-worn gunmetal plate armor. His closed helmet completely conceals his face, with no skin visible beneath the armor. He carries a shield and a long-handled axe, giving him the imposing but dependable presence of the party's defensive frontline.
