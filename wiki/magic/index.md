# Magic

Magic in Terra is associated with the use of **Aethra**, the fundamental force present throughout the world and living beings. Different peoples and institutions study and practice magic through their own traditions.

Magic is not universal. Its use depends on aptitude, sensitivity to Aethra, training, and the traditions available to the practitioner. Magic remains distinct from Race, Natural Evolution, and recognition by a [Sigil]({{ '/wiki/systems/sigils/' | relative_url }}).

## Fundamental Affinities

Magic aptitude in Terra is commonly described through exactly six Fundamental Affinities: **Fire, Wind, Water, Earth, Light, and Dark**. Affinity describes natural compatibility or potential. It does not determine character, allegiance, morality, or automatic magical skill.

### Material Affinities

The four Material Affinities are:

- Fire
- Wind
- Water
- Earth

They form a conceptual compatibility square. Fire opposes Water, while Wind opposes Earth.

### Understanding the Diagram

- Solid side connections indicate **adjacent affinities** with **moderate compatibility**.
- Dashed diagonal connections indicate **opposing affinities** with **very low compatibility**.
- Fire is adjacent to Wind and Earth, and opposes Water.
- Water is adjacent to Wind and Earth, and opposes Fire.
- Wind is adjacent to Fire and Water, and opposes Earth.
- Earth is adjacent to Fire and Water, and opposes Wind.

Adjacency does not grant an additional affinity. A Fire-affinity caster does not automatically possess Wind or Earth magic. Opposition is also not an absolute prohibition: an opposing affinity is generally harder to learn, less efficient, and more difficult to control, but not inherently impossible.

The diagram represents qualitative natural compatibility. It is not a skill tree, a guarantee of ability, a measure of spell strength, or a system of fixed numerical percentages.

### Metaphysical Affinities

**Light** and **Dark** form a separate metaphysical axis rather than belonging to the Material Affinity square. They oppose one another:

- Light ↔ Dark

Light is commonly associated with restoration, purification, and protection. Dark is associated with absorption, transfer, and weakening. Neither affinity determines morality: Light is not inherently good, and Dark is not inherently evil.

### Affinity and Mastery

Possessing an affinity does not make a person skilled in it. Affinity indicates natural potential and compatibility, while practical magic still depends on training, control, experience, sensitivity to Aethra, and technique.

A person may have a strong affinity while remaining an inexperienced caster. A less-compatible affinity may also be studied, but doing so is generally more difficult and inefficient.

### Multiple Affinities

Some individuals may possess more than one Fundamental Affinity, but this is not universal and does not automatically indicate exceptional power, mastery, or special status. Compatibility can influence how easily different affinities are developed together. For example, Wind and Water are generally easier to reconcile than Fire and Water.

## Advanced Expressions

- Water may develop into **Ice**.
- Wind may develop into **Lightning**.

Ice and Lightning are advanced expressions rather than Fundamental Affinities. Possessing Water or Wind affinity does not automatically grant the corresponding advanced expression; greater control, training, aptitude, or specialization is required.

## Composite Magic

Multiple affinities may combine into Composite Magic. The confirmed public example is:

- Fire + Earth → **Magma / Lava**

Magma/Lava is not a Fundamental Affinity. No complete combination matrix is currently recorded.

## Magic in Everyday Life

Magic is used beyond combat. Practical Ice magic, for example, can support refrigeration, food storage, fishing logistics, medicine, and trade. Specific practices vary between regions and institutions.
