# Forest Boar

The **Forest Boar** is a large, compact wild boar found around the Forest Edge and Outer Forest. Its heavy frame is supported by extremely strong shoulders and neck muscles, while coarse dark-brown bristles and thick hide protect much of its body.

## Strength and Attacks

The Forest Boar's primary attack is a direct **charge**. It uses its body mass and powerful shoulders to break through resistance, then brings its prominent curved tusks into close range. Its thick hide also makes a poorly placed counterattack less effective than it would be against lighter forest wildlife.

Its danger comes from natural strength, momentum, aggression, and tusks rather than an inherent magical attack.

## Size

An adult reference specimen stands approximately **1.3 metres at the shoulder**. Natural variation between individuals is expected.
