# Forest Wolf

The **Forest Wolf** is a large woodland predator found along the Forest Edge and through the Outer Forest. It has a lean, athletic build, long legs, strong shoulders, alert ears, and dark grey-brown fur that blends naturally with woodland terrain.

## Strength and Attacks

Forest Wolves rely on speed, stalking, and rapid close-range attacks rather than visible magic. Their jaws are their most direct weapon, while their claws are strong enough to tear exposed coverings and damage the wooden exterior of a caravan during an attack.

Their mobility makes them especially dangerous to isolated travelers and anyone unable to maintain a secure defensive position. No inherent magical attack is currently documented for an ordinary Forest Wolf.

## Size

An adult reference specimen stands approximately **1.2 metres at the shoulder**. Natural variation between individuals is expected.

## Appearance in the Novel

A Forest Wolf is recorded in **Volume I, Chapter 2 — “The City Without a Crown,”** during Luca's journey toward Arklune. This is its first confirmed placement in the current Volume I story record.
