# Bestiary

The Bestiary records wildlife and non-sapient creatures documented in the public archive. A creature's presence in the Monster Forest does not automatically imply magical powers, unnatural origin, or allegiance to Demonkind.

The name **Monster Forest** is a common Human geographic term. Sapient peoples who live in or near the forest are documented under [Races]({{ '/wiki/races/' | relative_url }}), not treated as monsters solely because of where they live.
