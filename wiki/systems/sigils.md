# Sigils and Authority

Sigils are recognition-based Authorities. They are separate from Race, Natural Evolution, and Class.

## Rune Sigils and Heroes

Rune Sigils are ancient recognition-based Authorities. A recognized bearer may hold the title **Hero**. Hero is not a race or Natural Evolution form.

Current public records do not establish a universal biological eligibility rule for Rune Sigils.

[Garling]({{ '/wiki/characters/garling/' | relative_url }}) is a publicly known Human Hero and Rune Sigil bearer from the Beta War.

## Demon Sigils and Demon Lords

Exactly seven Demon Sigils are known: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega. A recognized bearer may hold the title **Demon Lord**. Demon Lord is not a race or Natural Evolution form.

Complete eligibility for Demon Sigil recognition is not publicly established. The term Demonkind must not be used as a biological eligibility rule.

[Raizen]({{ '/wiki/characters/raizen/' | relative_url }}) is publicly recorded as Demon Lord Beta during the Beta War.
