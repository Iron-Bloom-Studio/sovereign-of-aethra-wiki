# Sigils

Sigils are recognized Authorities whose bearers have historically shaped major political domains. They are separate from Race, Natural Evolution, Class, and political affiliation.

## Rune Sigils and Heroes

Rune Sigils are recognition-based Authorities. A recognized bearer may hold the title **Hero**; Hero is not a race or Natural Evolution form.

Current public records do not establish a universal biological eligibility rule for Rune Sigils.

[Garling]({{ '/wiki/characters/garling/' | relative_url }}) is a publicly known Human Hero and Rune Sigil bearer from the Beta War.

## Known Sigils

Exactly seven Demon Sigils are known: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega. They are Ancient Authorities.

## Bearers and Domains

> A Demon Lord is one whose Dominion has been recognized by one of the Seven Sigils.

A recognized bearer may hold the title **Demon Lord**, which is not a race, Natural Evolution form, Class, faction, civilization, or political state. A Sigil, its bearer, and a political domain associated with that bearer remain distinct concepts.

Complete eligibility and Sigil-specific Recognition criteria are not publicly established. Historical association with a population does not establish biological ownership or eligibility, and political inheritance is separate from Authority Recognition.

[Raizen]({{ '/wiki/characters/raizen/' | relative_url }}) is publicly recorded as Demon Lord Beta during the Beta War.

## The Seven Sigils

[The Seven Sigils]({{ '/wiki/factions/seven-sigils/' | relative_url }}) is the geopolitical Great Power associated with the seven known Sigil domains and political structures. This political classification is separate from the Sigils themselves as Authorities.
