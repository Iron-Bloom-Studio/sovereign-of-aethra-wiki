# Sigils and Authority

Sigils are recognition-based Authorities. They are separate from Race, Natural Evolution, and Class.

## Rune Sigils and Heroes

Rune Sigils are ancient recognition-based Authorities. A recognized bearer may hold the title **Hero**. Hero is not a race or Natural Evolution form.

Conceptually, this relationship is: **Ideal / Virtue → Recognition → Hero**.

Current public records do not establish a universal biological eligibility rule for Rune Sigils.

[Garling]({{ '/wiki/characters/garling/' | relative_url }}) is a publicly known Human Hero and Rune Sigil bearer from the Beta War.

## Demon Sigils and Demon Lords

Exactly seven Demon Sigils are known: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega. They are Ancient Authorities.

Conceptually, this relationship is: **Dominion → Recognition → Demon Lord**.

> A Demon Lord is one whose Dominion has been recognized by one of the Seven Demon Sigils.

Dominion is not synonymous with conquest. A recognized bearer may hold the title **Demon Lord**, which is not a race, Natural Evolution form, Class, faction, civilization, or political state.

Complete eligibility and Sigil-specific Recognition criteria are not publicly established. The term Demonkind must not be used as a biological eligibility rule. Demon Lord Authority is not automatically hereditary, and political inheritance is separate from Authority Recognition.

[Raizen]({{ '/wiki/characters/raizen/' | relative_url }}) is publicly recorded as Demon Lord Beta during the Beta War.
