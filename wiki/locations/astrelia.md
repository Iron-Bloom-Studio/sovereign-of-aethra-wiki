# Astrelia

**Astrelia** is one of the five Human kingdoms of the Grand Concord and is commonly known as the **Magic Kingdom**. Its capital, **Myra**, is associated with advanced magical education, formal Aethra study, and institutions devoted to refining the practical use of magic.

## History
Astrelia's identity developed around generations of organized magical scholarship. Where many cultures preserved magic through family, guild, temple, battlefield, or local tradition, Astrelia increasingly treated magical knowledge as something that could be recorded, compared, taught, and improved systematically.

Astrelian forces eventually participated in the final stages of the Battle of Asteron, alongside the wider coalition resisting Demon Lord Beta.

## Capital — Myra
**Myra** is the kingdom's political and magical center. Academies, laboratories, libraries, workshops, and regulated magical facilities form an important part of the capital's identity.

Its royal architecture should express disciplined magical civilization rather than a city made entirely from impossible floating towers or uncontrolled spectacle.

## Government
Astrelia is ruled in the present era by **Queen Lyra**. The Crown governs alongside influential magical institutions and learned officials, creating a political culture in which magical expertise can carry significant authority.

## Society and Culture
Magic is prestigious but not universal. Astrelian society includes farmers, craftsmen, soldiers, merchants, administrators, and ordinary citizens alongside Mages and researchers.

The kingdom tends to respect demonstrable magical ability and formal study, though this can also create social divisions between prestigious institutions and those outside them.

## Military
Astrelia fields conventional forces supported by trained magical specialists. Battlefield magic is treated as part of coordinated warfare rather than a replacement for infantry, logistics, fortifications, and command.

## Aethra and Magic
Astrelia is one of the Human world's foremost centers for formal study of Aethra and spellcraft. Its scholars examine magical structures, efficiency, control, and repeatability, while acknowledging that much about Aethra remains unknown.

## Economy
Magical education, enchanted or Aethra-assisted crafts, specialist services, agriculture, and inter-kingdom trade all contribute to Astrelia's economy.

## Relations
Astrelia is a sovereign member of the Grand Concord. Its scholars often exchange knowledge with Eldoria, while its magical institutions may approach Aethra differently from the traditions of Sylvaris or other non-Human civilizations.

## Present Day
Astrelia remains a center of Human magical learning. Its greatest strength is not that it has solved Aethra, but that it has built institutions dedicated to asking increasingly precise questions about it.
