# Eldoria

**Eldoria** is one of the five Human kingdoms of the Grand Concord and is widely known as the **Kingdom of Knowledge**. Its capital, **Arden**, is a major center of scholarship, administration, historical study, and practical learning. Compared with many neighboring realms, Eldoria has a reputation for relative openness toward travelers and non-Human peoples, especially in its larger towns and southern frontier.

## History
Eldoria developed around communities that placed unusual value on records, schools, archives, and trained civil administration. Over time, this culture produced a kingdom whose influence rested as much on knowledge and organization as on military force.

Fort Asteron once guarded Eldoria's southern approaches. During the Beta War, it became the site of the devastating **Battle of Asteron**, where forces from several Human kingdoms and Sylvaris resisted the army of Demon Lord Beta. The battle ended in victory, but the surrounding region suffered severe Aethra Distortion and Asteron was eventually abandoned.

The difficulty of coordinating the Human kingdoms during the crisis later became one of the historical arguments for closer cooperation among the Five Crowns.

## Capital — Arden
**Arden** is the political and intellectual heart of Eldoria. Its identity is shaped by libraries, academies, archives, guild halls, government offices, and old districts built around institutions of learning.

The royal seat is expected to reflect Eldoria's character: dignified rather than extravagant, with architecture emphasizing permanence, records, deliberation, and public administration.

## Government
Eldoria is a monarchy ruled in the present era by **King Edric**. The Crown governs alongside an established administrative and scholarly class whose influence is strongest in law, education, archives, infrastructure, and state planning.

Eldoria is also one of the five sovereign members of the **Grand Concord**. Membership in the Concord does not remove the kingdom's sovereignty.

## Society and Culture
Education carries considerable social prestige. Scholars, surveyors, healers, administrators, engineers, historians, and experienced adventurers may all earn respect through demonstrated expertise.

Eldorian society is not free of prejudice, but its major settlements are generally more accustomed to outsiders than many traditional Human regions. This has helped make its southern frontier an important meeting ground between peoples.

## Military
Eldoria's armed forces traditionally emphasize organized defense, fortified positions, trained officers, logistics, and coordination. The kingdom's experience at Asteron remains an important part of its military memory.

## Aethra and Learning
Eldorian study of Aethra tends to be practical and comparative. Rather than treating magic as a single isolated discipline, its scholars often record how Aethra interacts with history, ecology, medicine, ruins, and different peoples.

## Economy
Eldoria benefits from agriculture, skilled crafts, education, administration, trade routes, and services connected to scholarship and adventuring. Its southern routes also connect the kingdom to the frontier and the Free City of Arklune.

## Relations
Eldoria maintains formal ties with the other four Human kingdoms through the Grand Concord. It also has significant contact with **Arklune**, whose independence and multiracial culture make the city both useful and politically unusual.

## Notable Locations
- **Arden** — capital of Eldoria.
- **Aven Village** — a settlement within Eldoria and the home of Luca at the beginning of the present-day story.
- **Fort Asteron / Asteron** — abandoned southern stronghold and site of the Battle of Asteron.
- **Southern Frontier** — a broad frontier region connecting Eldoria to Arklune, the Monster Forest, and lands farther south.

## Present Day
Eldoria remains one of the principal Human realms of Terra. Its reputation for knowledge and relative openness makes it a natural crossroads between the older Human kingdoms and the increasingly interconnected world beyond them.
