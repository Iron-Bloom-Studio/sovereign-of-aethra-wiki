# Free City of Arklune

The **Free City of Arklune** is an independent, multiracial city-state situated near the southern frontier of Eldoria. Although closely connected to Eldorian trade and travel, Arklune is not a city of the Eldorian Crown. It is a sovereign enclave whose identity was built around freedom of movement, earned reputation, and the practical cooperation of people from many backgrounds.

Its most famous institution is the **Grand Adventurer Guild**, whose headquarters draws adventurers, merchants, scholars, craftsmen, mercenaries, explorers, and travelers from across Terra.

## Identity
Arklune is often associated with a simple civic ideal: **no king, no crown, and no race inherently above another**. In practice, the city is imperfect, crowded, competitive, and sometimes dangerous, but status is more readily earned through deeds and reputation than through noble birth.

This makes Arklune unusual among the established powers of Terra.

## History
Arklune grew where frontier roads, adventuring routes, trade, and dangerous wilderness met. Its usefulness to surrounding realms allowed the settlement to develop a degree of autonomy that eventually became a defining political fact.

The city survived by being valuable to many powers without becoming the possession of one. The rise of the Grand Adventurer Guild further strengthened its international character.

## Government
Arklune is a free city rather than a hereditary monarchy. Its exact civic institutions may include guild and civic representation, but the final constitutional structure remains under development.

The Grand Adventurer Guild is enormously influential, but it should not be treated as identical to the city government.

## The Grand Adventurer Guild
Arklune serves as the headquarters of the **Grand Adventurer Guild**, one of Terra's most widely recognized adventuring institutions. The Guild coordinates contracts, rankings, expeditions, monster suppression, escorts, exploration, and other work that crosses political borders.

Its neutral character is one reason Arklune attracts people who would face greater restrictions elsewhere.

## Society and Culture
Human, Elf, Dwarf, and other peoples can be encountered in Arklune. The city is particularly attractive to adventurers, refugees, independent craftsmen, itinerant scholars, and people seeking a new beginning.

Prejudice still exists among individuals and communities, but Arklune's culture rewards competence and useful contribution strongly enough to create spaces that are uncommon elsewhere.

## Economy
Arklune thrives on adventuring, trade, inns, equipment, repair work, transport, information, monster materials, frontier supplies, and services for travelers. Its markets reflect the variety of people who pass through the city.

## Military and Security
Arklune depends on civic guards, experienced adventurers, and organized emergency response rather than the standing army of a large kingdom. The presence of the Grand Adventurer Guild gives the city access to exceptional individual fighters, but also requires careful coordination during major crises.

## Relations
Arklune maintains especially important ties with **Eldoria**, while preserving its independence. Its neutral and multiracial character also gives it connections extending well beyond the Human kingdoms.

## Notable Locations
- **Grand Adventurer Guild Headquarters** — the city's most famous institution.
- **Guild District** — services and businesses centered on adventurers and travelers.
- **Market and Caravan Districts** — major points of regional exchange.
- **Southern Roads** — routes toward Asteron, the Monster Forest, and other frontier territories.

## Present Day
Arklune remains one of the best places in Terra for a young adventurer to encounter a world larger than their homeland. It is a city where histories, races, ambitions, and conflicts meet before moving outward again.
