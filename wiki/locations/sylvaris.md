# Sylvaris

**Sylvaris** is the principal Elven realm of Terra and the heart of the Great Power known as the **Wardens of the Great Tree**. Its capital, **Elyra**, lies within the immense **Great Forest**, an ecosystem whose age and complexity predate many modern states.

## History
Elven civilization in Sylvaris developed in close relationship with the Great Forest and the natural movement of Aethra through living systems. Its historical records reach far into the past, though even Elven archives are not complete and not every accepted tradition is necessarily the final truth of history.

Sylvaris sent an elite contingent to aid the defense of Asteron during the Beta War. The Spellblade and Aethra Scholar **Eldren** was among the Elves present during the battle.

## Capital — Elyra
**Elyra** is the political and cultural center of Sylvaris. Its architecture should coexist with the forest rather than simply replace it: living trees, elevated structures, stone, flowing water, bridges, gardens, and carefully shaped spaces may all form part of the city.

The **Great Tree** is an ancient sacred Aethra landmark of enormous importance to Elven civilization. Its deepest nature remains a subject of study and tradition rather than settled public fact.

## Government
Sylvaris is ruled in the present era by **King Aelar**, alongside an influential **Elder Council**. **First Elder Thalen** represents a more conservative current within Elven politics.

The realm also contains old noble Houses and lineages whose exact roles continue to be developed.

## Society and Culture
Elven culture places strong value on continuity, memory, stewardship, skill, and the long consequences of decisions. Long lifespans shape politics and personal relationships differently from Human societies.

Not all Elves follow the same path. **High Elf** and **Dark Elf** are Natural Evolution branches rather than moral categories; neither is inherently good or evil.

## Military
Sylvaris favors forces suited to forests, mobility, precision, scouting, archery, magic, and highly trained specialists. Its military traditions are adapted to defending a living environment rather than massing armies on open plains.

## Aethra
Elven understanding of Aethra is closely connected to ecology, living systems, and long observation. This does not mean Elves possess complete knowledge of Aethra.

## Ancient Rune Sigils
Sylvaris is known to guard **two ancient Rune Sigils**. Their identities remain TBD for public canon.

## Economy
Forest products gathered under controlled traditions, fine crafts, medicine, specialized magical goods, and limited external trade contribute to the realm's economy.

## Relations
Sylvaris maintains relations with the Human kingdoms while protecting its sovereignty and the Great Forest. The emergence of the **Rogue Hive** led by Zavor has created a serious security problem within or near the forest, without making The Hive as a civilization responsible for his actions.

## Notable Locations
- **Elyra** — capital of Sylvaris.
- **Great Forest** — immense forest ecosystem and Elven homeland.
- **Great Tree** — ancient sacred Aethra landmark.
- Two guarded Rune sites — names TBD.

## Present Day
Sylvaris remains one of Terra's oldest major civilizations, balancing preservation with a changing world. Questions about history, the Great Forest, and the nature of Aethra continue to shape its future.
