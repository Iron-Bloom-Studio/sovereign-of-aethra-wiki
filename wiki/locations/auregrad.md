# Auregrad

**Auregrad** is one of the five Human kingdoms of the Grand Concord and is known in the present era as the **Military Kingdom**. Its capital is **Varen**, and its ruler, **Emperor Kael**, currently holds the coordinating office of Emperor within the Grand Concord.

The title does not make Kael sovereign over the other four Human kingdoms. Each Crown remains independent.

## History
Auregrad was not always the military power it is today. During the Battle of Asteron, its forces arrived as conventional cavalry, infantry, and Knights. The kingdom did not yet possess the modern military institutions for which it would later become famous, and the **Wyvern Corps did not exist** at that time.

The generations following the Beta War saw Auregrad place increasing emphasis on readiness, organization, mobility, and large-scale defense. The exact relationship between Asteron and individual later reforms is complex, but the battle remains part of the historical context in which Human military thinking changed.

## Capital — Varen
**Varen** is a disciplined capital shaped by government, military institutions, training grounds, logistics, and ceremonial displays of state power. Its royal seat should feel formidable and ordered rather than needlessly brutal.

## Government
Auregrad is ruled by **Emperor Kael**. "Emperor" refers to his current coordinating position within the Grand Concord as well as Auregrad's political prestige; it does not abolish the sovereignty of the other Crowns.

## Society and Culture
Discipline, public service, preparedness, and martial achievement carry considerable prestige. However, Auregrad is still a complete society of farmers, artisans, families, merchants, officials, scholars, and soldiers—not a nation composed only of warriors.

## Military
Auregrad fields one of the most developed Human military systems of the present era. Its most famous modern formation is the **Wyvern Corps**.

The breeding and military use of Wyverns is a major source of tension with **Dragonkind**, who regard Wyverns as beings of draconic blood rather than mere military animals.

## Aethra and Warfare
Auregrad tends to approach Aethra through battlefield utility, defense, training, equipment, and coordinated doctrine rather than purely theoretical study.

## Economy
Military production, agriculture, crafts, transport, state logistics, and regional trade support the kingdom's large institutions.

## Relations
Auregrad is a leading member of the Grand Concord. Its relationship with the **Dragon Throne** is strained by the Wyvern issue, while its military weight gives it substantial influence in Human collective defense.

## Present Day
Auregrad represents the most visible military face of the Human kingdoms. Its challenge is not merely to become stronger, but to determine how that strength should be used in a world where Human security intersects with the sovereignty and dignity of other peoples.
