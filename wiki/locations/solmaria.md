# Solmaria

**Solmaria** is one of the five Human kingdoms of the Grand Concord and is commonly called the **Holy Kingdom**. Its capital, **Lumen**, is known for sacred institutions, healing traditions, ceremonial life, and a strong cultural emphasis on duty, mercy, and moral responsibility.

"Holy" in Solmaria does not mean that the kingdom possesses complete knowledge of Aethra or the divine. Its traditions are interpretations developed by people living within Terra, and different cultures understand the world in different ways.

## History
Solmaria developed around religious and charitable institutions that became increasingly important to social life, healing, education, and political legitimacy.

During the Beta War, Solmarian forces were among the reinforcements sent to Asteron. Healers and support personnel were particularly valuable during the prolonged defense.

## Capital — Lumen
**Lumen** is both royal capital and spiritual center. Temples, hospitals, hospices, plazas, archives of doctrine, and institutions of service contribute to its character.

Its architecture should communicate light, order, solemnity, and refuge without implying that Solmaria alone possesses cosmic truth.

## Government
Solmaria is ruled in the present era by **Queen Sera**. The Crown exists alongside influential religious institutions whose exact constitutional authority remains to be developed.

## Society and Culture
Service, charity, healing, vows, pilgrimage, ceremony, and communal obligation are important cultural themes. Solmarian society can be compassionate without being uniformly tolerant, and doctrinal disagreements exist within its institutions.

## Military
Solmaria maintains conventional forces supported by disciplined healers, protective magic, chaplains or equivalent spiritual officers, and logistical institutions experienced in caring for the wounded.

## Aethra and Faith
Solmarian traditions often interpret Aethra through moral, spiritual, and philosophical language. This differs from Astrelia's more formal magical scholarship or Eldoria's comparative academic traditions.

Ancient Rune Sigils associated with ideals such as **Mercy** or **Faith** may have connections to Solmarian territory, though exact identities and locations remain TBD.

## Economy
Agriculture, pilgrimage, healing institutions, crafts, education, and regional trade contribute to Solmaria's economy.

## Relations
Solmaria is a sovereign member of the Grand Concord. Its institutions can serve as sources of humanitarian aid and mediation, while differences in belief may also create tension with cultures that interpret Aethra and ancient powers differently.

## Present Day
Solmaria remains one of the most influential centers of Human spiritual life. Its people continue to debate what duty, mercy, faith, and responsibility mean in a world shared by many races and powers.
