# Beta War

The **Beta War** was a major conflict approximately **43 years before the main story**. The invasion was led by [Raizen]({{ '/wiki/characters/raizen/' | relative_url }}), the Oni King recognized as **Demon Lord Beta**, and threatened the southern frontier of the Human kingdom of [Eldoria]({{ '/wiki/locations/eldoria/' | relative_url }}).

Its best-documented and decisive confrontation was the **Battle of Asteron**, a seven-day defense of Fort Asteron by an emergency coalition of Human and Elven forces. The defenders stopped the invasion, but the victory devastated Asteron and permanently altered the surrounding flow of [Aethra]({{ '/wiki/world/aethra/' | relative_url }}).

The complete released narrative is available in the [Indonesian Prologue]({{ '/wiki/novel/id/prologue/' | relative_url }}) and [English Prologue]({{ '/wiki/novel/en/prologue/' | relative_url }}).

## Beta War and the Battle of Asteron

The names are related but not identical:

- **Beta War** refers to the wider conflict and invasion associated with Demon Lord Beta.
- **Battle of Asteron** refers to the seven-day battle at Fort Asteron that ended the invasion.

Fort Asteron was not merely a military outpost. It was a fortified Eldorian frontier settlement occupied by soldiers and civilians. When the invading army reached the region, evacuation and defense took place at the same time.

The five Human kingdoms already existed as independent sovereign states, but the [Grand Concord]({{ '/wiki/factions/grand-concord/' | relative_url }}) had not yet been formed. The defense of Asteron was therefore an emergency coalition rather than the operation of a permanent unified alliance.

## Seven-Day Chronology

| Day | Documented developments |
| --- | --- |
| **Day One** | The Beta army reached Asteron. Evacuation began while the defenders mounted their first resistance. |
| **Day Two** | Rolan reorganized the surviving defenders and established a more effective defensive command. |
| **Day Three** | Reinforcements from Solmaria and Verenza arrived. Their arrival strengthened Asteron, but differences in authority and command made coordination difficult. |
| **Day Four** | Attrition intensified. The defenders recognized that the invading army was being directed by an intelligent commander who had not yet entered the battlefield. |
| **Day Five** | Auregrad's conventional cavalry, infantry, and Knights arrived. Their counterattack was anticipated, baited, and defeated by the Beta army. |
| **Day Six** | Asteron's fortifications approached collapse. Eldren detected increasingly abnormal Aethra behavior as the invading army prepared for its ruler's arrival. |
| **Day Seven** | Raizen entered the battlefield and the western wall fell. Garling, Rolan, and Eldren confronted him together. Raizen was killed, the Beta army withdrew, and the defenders began evacuating when Aethra around Asteron destabilized. |

## Outcome

### End of the Invasion

Garling, Rolan, and Eldren combined their different strengths against Raizen. Their coordinated attack killed Demon Lord Beta on the seventh day. With its ruler dead, the invading army retreated south and the immediate invasion of Eldoria ended.

The victory came after severe losses and the destruction of much of Fort Asteron. The coalition did not pursue the retreating army because the surviving soldiers and civilians had to evacuate the region.

### Aethra Distortion and the Aethra Scar

After Raizen's death, accumulated magic, battlefield deaths, Rune Sigil power, and Demon Sigil power coincided with a severe disruption in the local flow of Aethra. The phenomenon became known as **Aethra Distortion**.

Magic around Asteron became unstable. Water and agriculture became unreliable, monster behavior changed, and long-term settlement grew increasingly difficult. Eldoria made later attempts to maintain or reclaim the region, but permanent habitation proved unsustainable. The territory surrounding the ruined fort eventually became known as the **Aethra Scar**, and Asteron was abandoned.

The exact mechanism behind the distortion remains unexplained in the public record.

### Political Legacy

Asteron demonstrated that the independent Human kingdoms could fight together, but also exposed the cost of slow reinforcement, conflicting authority, and the absence of a shared command structure. In the postwar period, the five kingdoms—Astrelia, Verenza, Solmaria, Eldoria, and Auregrad—created the **Grand Concord** to improve coordination against future large-scale threats.

The Grand Concord did not merge the five kingdoms into one empire. Each kingdom retained its sovereignty, while the new institution provided a framework for cooperation.

The battle also influenced later Human military thinking. Auregrad, which had reached Asteron with conventional forces and suffered under the limits of that system, placed increasing emphasis on readiness, organization, mobility, and large-scale defense in subsequent generations.

## Limits of Participation

- The [Dwarven Holds]({{ '/wiki/factions/dwarven-holds/' | relative_url }}) did **not officially join** the defense, although individual Dwarves may have acted independently.
- [Dragonkind]({{ '/wiki/races/dragonkind/' | relative_url }}) remained independent and did not join either side.
- Hiveborn were unknown to Asteron's defenders and were not part of the conflict.
- The defending coalition was not yet the Grand Concord.

## Historical Importance

The Beta War is remembered as both a military victory and a warning. The invasion was stopped, but Fort Asteron could not be saved as a permanent settlement. The battle reshaped Human cooperation, altered Eldoria's southern frontier, and left an Aethra disturbance that remained unresolved decades later.
