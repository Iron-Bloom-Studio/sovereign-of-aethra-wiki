# The Grand Concord

The Grand Concord is an alliance of five sovereign Human kingdoms, governed through a Council of Five Crowns. Its Emperor is a coordinator, not an absolute ruler.

Astrelia, Verenza, Solmaria, Eldoria, and Auregrad form the alliance.

## Heraldry Standard

Grand Concord heraldry uses two distinct presentation levels:

- **Official / Ceremonial Banner** — may use the detailed, ornamental, and luxurious treatments shown in the founding-ceremony artwork.
- **Standard / Field Banner** — uses the simplified established emblem for clear recognition in ordinary illustrations, uniforms, maps, and battlefield scenes.

| Kingdom | Standard / Field Emblem |
|---|---|
| Eldoria | Open book + star |
| Astrelia | Aethra Star |
| Verenza | Scales |
| Solmaria | Sun |
| Auregrad | Wyvern |
