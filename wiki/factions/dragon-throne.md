# The Dragon Throne

The **Dragon Throne** is the Great Power of **Dragonkind**, centered on the vast **Great Dragon Mountains**. It is one of Terra's oldest and most formidable political powers, ruled in the present era by **Dragon Emperor Rhaen**, a True Dragon associated with the Dark element.

Dark Aethra or a Dark elemental affinity is not inherently evil. Rhaen is known as a patient and deliberate ruler rather than a figure defined by Human ideas of darkness.

## Territory
The Great Dragon Mountains form the heartland of Dragonkind. Their peaks, valleys, caverns, high plateaus, and ancient routes are difficult for outsiders to traverse without permission or preparation.

The Dragon Throne does not currently use "Drakara" as the name of its civilization or capital. Any older project references to Drakara are deprecated.

The formal capital or primary Seat of Power of the Dragon Throne remains **TBD**.

## History
Dragonkind has existed as an independent power across eras of Human and Demonkind conflict. During the Battle of Asteron, Dragonkind did not join either side. This neutrality was not mysterious to contemporary powers: Dragonkind was known to act according to its own sovereignty, territorial interests, and understanding of balance.

## Government
**Dragon Emperor Rhaen** stands at the center of the Dragon Throne. He is advised or balanced by the **Dragon Conclave**, a council associated with powerful Dragon Lords.

Known or developing members include:
- **Ignar** — Fire.
- **Lumera** — Light.
- Additional Lords associated with Lightning, Water/Ice, Earth, and Wind remain PROVISIONAL.

The exact legal relationship between Emperor, Conclave, territories, and individual Dragon Lords remains under development.

## Society and Culture
Dragonkind should not be understood simply as solitary beasts. The Dragon Throne represents an old civilization with hierarchy, memory, territorial customs, obligations, and political relationships extending beyond Human lifetimes.

Long life changes the meaning of promises, grudges, borders, and historical memory within Dragon society.

## Dragon and Wyvern Lineages
**Dragons** and **Wyverns** are separate draconic lineages within the broader Dragonkind framework. Wyverns are not merely animals in the eyes of Dragonkind.

This is the basis of a major present-day dispute with **Auregrad**, whose military breeds and uses Wyverns in the Wyvern Corps.

## Military Power
Dragonkind's strength rests in powerful individuals, aerial dominance, elemental abilities, difficult terrain, and the ability of the Dragon Throne to mobilize ancient lineages when necessary.

The civilization should not be depicted as constantly seeking conquest. Its power is often expressed through deterrence and the fact that other states must consider how Dragonkind will respond.

## Aethra
Dragonkind possesses ancient traditions concerning Aethra and elemental development. A Dragon's element does not determine moral alignment.

The Natural Evolution path from Dragon toward **True Dragon** is currently PROVISIONAL within the wider evolution framework.

## Economy and Exchange
Dragonkind is comparatively selective about outside access, but exchange with neighboring civilizations does occur. Mountain resources, rare materials, knowledge, and controlled routes may all play roles in these relationships.

## Relations
- **Auregrad** — significant tension over Wyvern breeding and military use.
- **Dwarven Holds** — coexistence and exchange shaped by shared proximity to the Great Dragon Mountains.
- Other powers — treated according to sovereignty, conduct, territory, and long-term interest rather than automatic Human/Demon alignment.

## Notable Locations
- **Great Dragon Mountains** — Dragonkind heartland.
- **Seat of the Dragon Throne** — name TBD.
- Domains of individual Dragon Lords — TBD.

## Present Day
The Dragon Throne remains one of Terra's strongest powers and one of its least easily pressured. Its involvement in world affairs is selective, which makes every formal intervention politically significant.
