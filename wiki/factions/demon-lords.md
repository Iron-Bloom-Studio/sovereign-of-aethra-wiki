# Demon Lords

<p class="lede">The recognized bearers associated with the seven known Sigils.</p>

A **Demon Lord** is one whose Dominion has been recognized by one of the [Seven Sigils]({{ '/wiki/factions/seven-sigils/' | relative_url }}). The seven titles — Demon Lord Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega — are publicly known. A Sigil, its bearer, and any associated political domain remain distinct.

Where no confirmed public portrait is available, a placeholder silhouette is shown. This page records only publicly released details.

## Public Briefs

### Demon Lord Alpha

Details unknown.

### Raizen — Demon Lord Beta

Raizen is recorded as the Oni King and Demon Lord Beta during the Beta War. Public records of that war state that he was killed on its seventh day.

### Demon Lord Gamma

Details unknown.

### Rex — Demon Lord Delta

Rex is publicly known as Demon Lord Delta, a recognized bearer of the Delta Sigil.

### Demon Lord Theta

Demon Lord Theta holds a Poseidon-like authority over the seas, including currents, tidal walls, storms and pressure, along with Leviathan-related powers.

### Demon Lord Sigma

Details unknown.

### Demon Lord Omega

Details unknown.
