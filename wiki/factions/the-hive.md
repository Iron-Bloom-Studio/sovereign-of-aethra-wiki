# The Hive

**The Hive** is the civilization of the **Hiveborn**, a people whose social and biological traditions differ sharply from those of the older Human, Elven, and Dwarven realms. In the present era, the **Zyrath Hive** is the dominant political Hive and one of Terra's recognized Great Powers.

The term "Hive" should not be mistaken for a single mind. Hiveborn possess individual identity, and **Hive Resonance** is not total mind control.

## Emergence in Recorded History
Hiveborn were unknown to the defenders of Asteron during the Beta War. Roughly two decades after the battle, reports began to spread of strange humanoid and insectoid peoples appearing in the far north.

Human and Elven observers initially lacked a clear classification for them, and some suspected a previously unknown form of Demonkind. Continued contact eventually established that the Hiveborn were a distinct people with their own society, biology, and political structures.

Their deeper origin remains uncertain in public knowledge.

## Zyrath Hive
The **Zyrath Hive** is the leading political Hive of the present era. "Zyrath" refers to this dominant political body rather than serving as the permanent name for all Hiveborn civilization.

The exact location and form of its principal seat remain under development.

## Society and Culture
Hiveborn biology includes strong concepts of function, caste, and specialized development. These roles influence society but should not be interpreted as proof that every Hiveborn lacks personal choice.

Hiveborn can experience loyalty, conflict, ambition, uncertainty, and individual purpose. The tension between assigned function and self-determined purpose is an important question within their civilization.

## Natural Evolution
Hiveborn Natural Evolution is closely associated with functional or caste-based specialization. Known conceptual directions include Warrior, Scout, and Worker forms, though final names and biology remain under development.

## Government
The political organization of the Zyrath Hive differs from Human monarchy. Its exact institutions, leadership titles, and relationship between Resonance and governance remain TBD.

## Military
Hiveborn forces can draw on specialized bodies and coordinated group behavior. Their strength should come from biological adaptation, organization, and complementary roles rather than portraying them as an unlimited swarm.

## Aethra
Hiveborn interact with Aethra through biological pathways that other peoples do not yet fully understand. Their evolutionary biology makes them particularly important to comparative study of Aethra and life.

## The Rogue Hive
**Zavor — The Severed** leads a Rogue Hive associated with the Great Forest/Sylvaris region. His faction is a threat to the forest and its Aethra ecology.

The Rogue Hive is **not** synonymous with the Zyrath Hive or Hiveborn civilization as a whole. The Hive should not be blamed collectively for Zavor's actions.

## Relations
The sudden historical appearance of Hiveborn created suspicion among older powers. In the present era, The Hive is recognized as a major civilization, although cultural misunderstandings remain common.

## Present Day
For Luca's generation, Hiveborn are part of the known political world. For older people who lived through the Beta War, their presence is a reminder that Terra can still reveal entire peoples absent from earlier maps.
