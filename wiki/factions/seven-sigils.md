# The Seven Demon Sigils

Exactly seven Demon Sigils exist: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega. A Demon Sigil is Authority recognition, not biological race or natural evolution.

Complete eligibility for recognition is not publicly established. Demonkind is not a biological eligibility category.
