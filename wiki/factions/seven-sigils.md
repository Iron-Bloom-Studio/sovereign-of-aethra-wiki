# The Seven Sigils

**The Seven Sigils** is the geopolitical Great Power associated with seven known Sigil domains and political structures: **Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega**.

It is one Great Power. Alpha through Omega are not classified as separate Great Powers.

## Great Power

The Seven Sigils is not a single race, biological lineage, or conventional kingdom. Its public classification reflects the collective geopolitical weight of the domains and political structures historically associated with the seven known Sigils.

Those domains may differ in population, organization, and political tradition. Their association with a Sigil does not establish biological ownership of that Authority.

## Sigils, Bearers, and Domains

A Sigil is an Authority. A **Demon Lord** is a recognized bearer or status. A **domain** is a political territory or structure associated with a Sigil or bearer. These concepts are related but are not interchangeable.

Public records do not establish a universal racial requirement for recognition. Political inheritance is also separate from Authority Recognition: a successor may inherit territory or institutions without automatically becoming a recognized bearer.

For the reader-safe overview of Sigils as Authorities, see [Sigils]({{ '/wiki/systems/sigils/' | relative_url }}).
