# The Seven Demon Sigils

The **Seven Demon Sigils** are seven ancient Authorities known in Terra: **Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega**.

An individual recognized by one of these Authorities may bear the title **Demon Lord**.

## Dominion

The Demon Sigils are associated with **Dominion**.

> A Demon Lord is one whose Dominion has been recognized by one of the Seven Demon Sigils.

Dominion is not simply conquest. It may take forms such as allegiance, loyalty, pact, protection, recognition, shared purpose, conquest, or another form appropriate to a particular Sigil. The specific Recognition criteria used by Alpha through Omega have not been established publicly.

## Demon Lords

A Demon Lord is a recognized bearer of a Demon Sigil. **Demon Lord is not a race**, Natural Evolution, Class, faction, civilization, or political state.

Demon Lord Authority is not automatically hereditary. A political successor may inherit territory, followers, institutions, an army, responsibilities, or political claims without inheriting the same Demon Sigil or title.

## Sigils and Political Domains

A Demon Sigil, its recognized bearer, and a political domain associated with that bearer are related but distinct concepts.

States, factions, tribes, or coalitions may continue after a bearer is gone even when the corresponding Authority has no recognized bearer.

## Race and Recognition

The peoples of Terra do not possess a complete understanding of every condition governing Demon Sigil Recognition. Historical association between a population and a Sigil is not proof of biological eligibility.

The term [Demonkind]({{ '/wiki/races/demonkind/' | relative_url }}) may describe historical, cultural, political, or social associations, but it is not a biological prerequisite for Recognition.

## Hero and Demon Lord

[Rune Sigils]({{ '/wiki/systems/sigils/' | relative_url }}) are associated with **Heroes** through recognition of an ideal or virtue. Demon Sigils are associated with **Demon Lords** through recognition of Dominion.

Neither title is a race, and neither system is an automatic classification of Good and Evil.

## Known Sigils

- Alpha
- Beta
- Gamma
- Delta
- Theta
- Sigma
- Omega

No Sigil-specific Recognition criteria are stated here because those rules remain unknown.

## Origins

The true origin of the Seven Demon Sigils is not currently known. Questions concerning their creation, deeper purpose, and complete rules remain unanswered.
