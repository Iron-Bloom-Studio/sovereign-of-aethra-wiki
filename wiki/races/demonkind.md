# Demonkind

Demonkind is an in-world historical, cultural, political, and social umbrella term. It is not one biological race, a biological superclass, or a parent branch of the Race Tree.

Public records may use the term for peoples, armies, territories, or political identities associated with the Demon Domains. Such usage does not establish shared biological ancestry or determine an individual's morality or allegiance.

Demon Sigils are Authorities rather than biological traits. Recognition by a Sigil and the title Demon Lord are not Natural Evolution.
