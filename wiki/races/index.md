# Races of Terra

Terra is home to many sapient peoples. A person's **race** describes biological lineage; it does not determine morality, political allegiance, profession, or destiny.

## Race and Natural Evolution

Some races can undergo [Natural Evolution]({{ '/wiki/systems/natural-evolution/' | relative_url }}), a permanent transformation of the body and its relationship with Aethra. Natural Evolution is separate from [Class]({{ '/wiki/systems/class/' | relative_url }}) progression and ancient Authority systems.

This Wiki shows only evolution paths already revealed to readers. Unknown or unrevealed branches are not filled speculatively.

## Race Is Not Authority

**Hero** and **Demon Lord** are not races.

- Rune Sigil recognition is associated with the title **Hero**.
- Demon Sigil recognition is associated with the title **Demon Lord**.

Sigil recognition is not biological evolution. See [Sigils and Authority]({{ '/wiki/systems/sigils/' | relative_url }}).

## Race Is Not Allegiance

A race is not automatically bound to a kingdom, faction, civilization, or political cause. Historical terms used within Terra may describe culture, politics, conflict, or incomplete understanding rather than biological taxonomy.

## Monsters and Peoples

The word *monster* does not automatically identify a biological race. The Wiki distinguishes social terminology from biological classification whenever that distinction is known.
