# Realms & Civilizations of Terra

<p class="lede">A guide to Terra's Great Powers, kingdoms, independent polities, organizations, Holds, domains, and major civilizations.</p>

The present era follows the [Beta War]({{ '/wiki/history/beta-war/' | relative_url }}), but peace has not erased historical distrust or political rivalry. Trade and diplomacy cross many borders, while cooperation and social integration vary greatly between regions. [Arklune]({{ '/wiki/locations/arklune/' | relative_url }}) is comparatively open to travelers of different backgrounds without representing conditions everywhere in Terra.
