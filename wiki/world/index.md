# World

**Terra** is the primary setting of *Sovereign of Aethra*. Its known lands include the Great Continent, varied regions and ecosystems, and societies shaped by long histories of migration, trade, diplomacy, and conflict.

This guide presents only currently released knowledge of the world and its observable phenomena.
