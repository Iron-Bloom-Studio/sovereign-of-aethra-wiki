# Aethra

**Aethra** is a fundamental force present throughout Terra and all known forms of life.

It flows through the natural world and can be found within living beings, the land, water, and atmosphere. Its presence makes magic possible and plays an important role in the growth, adaptation, and evolution of many species.

Despite centuries of observation and study, the true origin of Aethra remains unknown.

Different civilizations have developed their own ways of understanding and using it. Mages shape Aethra into magic, Healers use it to restore the body, and Spellblades channel it through weapons. Other peoples interact with Aethra through biological traits, inherited abilities, or traditions unique to their lineage and culture.

Aethra itself is neither inherently benevolent nor malevolent. The same force may be used to heal, create, protect, destroy, or conquer.

## Aethra in Nature

Aethra is not limited to those capable of consciously using magic. It exists throughout Terra's environment.

Its influence can be observed in living ecosystems, magical phenomena, and creatures whose biology has adapted to unusually strong concentrations of Aethra.

The amount, behavior, and apparent flow of Aethra can differ from one region to another.

Scholars continue to study these differences, but no single civilization possesses a complete understanding of how Aethra behaves across the entire world.

## Aethra and Living Beings

All known living beings interact with Aethra in some form, although their ability to consciously perceive or manipulate it varies greatly.

A trained Mage may deliberately shape it into a spell. A Healer may use it to assist the restoration of an injured body. A Spellblade may channel it through a weapon to reinforce an attack or technique.

Other creatures interact with Aethra instinctively rather than through formal magical training.

The relationship between Aethra and living beings is also one of the reasons scholars study the development and adaptation of different races.

## Magic

Magic is one of the most visible applications of Aethra.

Through training, talent, technique, and cultural knowledge, individuals can learn to manipulate Aethra to produce specific effects.

The exact methods vary widely between civilizations and disciplines.

A Mage may construct a spell directly. A Healer may guide Aethra through a wounded body. A Spellblade may combine martial technique with controlled Aethra flowing through a weapon.

Different cultures have developed their own theories, terminology, and practices, and there is no universally accepted explanation for every form of magic found on Terra.

## Natural Evolution

For some races, Aethra is connected to a phenomenon known as **Natural Evolution**.

Natural Evolution is a lasting transformation in which an individual develops beyond the ordinary form of its current lineage.

It is not simply a matter of gaining experience or reaching a specific age. Physical development, environment, lineage, experience, and compatibility with Aethra may all influence whether an evolution occurs.

Different races possess different known evolutionary paths. Some may have several possible branches. Others have no confirmed Natural Evolution at all.

Natural Evolution is distinct from a person's **Class**. A Class represents learned abilities, profession, combat role, or specialized training, while Natural Evolution changes the individual at a more fundamental biological and Aethra-related level.

Much about the phenomenon remains the subject of ongoing study.

## Aethra Distortion

Under extreme circumstances, the normal behavior of Aethra can become unstable or deviate from its established patterns.

This phenomenon is known as **Aethra Distortion**.

Distorted Aethra is not necessarily corrupted, poisoned, or inherently evil. Instead, its flow behaves in ways that differ from the patterns normally observed by Mages and Scholars.

Possible effects may include unstable magic, changes in local ecosystems, difficulty sustaining agriculture, unusual environmental behavior, and increased agitation or migration among nearby monsters.

The most famous documented example occurred after the **Battle of Asteron**.

The extraordinary concentration of magic, Aethra use, powerful Sigils, and death during the battle was followed by a severe change in the region's Aethra.

The resulting affected territory eventually became known as the **Aethra Scar**.

Even decades later, the exact mechanism behind the Asteron distortion remains unexplained.

## Study of Aethra

Aethra has been studied by many civilizations for generations.

Human academies, Elven scholars, magical practitioners, Adventurers, and other traditions have developed different methods for observing and interpreting it.

Some fields focus on practical magic. Others study Aethra's relationship with living beings, ecosystems, artifacts, or unusual geographical phenomena.

These traditions do not always agree.

Even fundamental questions remain unresolved.

Where does Aethra originate?

Why does its behavior differ between regions and species?

What determines the limits of its influence on living beings?

Modern scholarship can describe many of Aethra's observable effects.

It cannot yet explain Aethra completely.

## Known Limitations of Current Knowledge

Much of what is publicly understood about Aethra is based on observation rather than certainty.

Scholars can measure some of its effects. Mages can manipulate it. Healers can use it. Many species have adapted alongside it.

But these facts do not reveal its ultimate origin or the full extent of what Aethra may be capable of.

For the people of Terra, Aethra remains both an everyday reality and one of the world's oldest unanswered questions.
