# Terra

Terra is the current setting of *Sovereign of Aethra*: a living world whose Great Continent holds kingdoms, realms, free cities, and the southern Demon Domains.

## Major Regions

The publicly released map identifies the Human Kingdoms, Great Forest, Great Dragon Mountains, and southern Demon Domains among the major regions of the Great Continent.
