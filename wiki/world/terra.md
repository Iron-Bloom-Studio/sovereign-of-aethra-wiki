# Terra

**Terra** is the world in which *Sovereign of Aethra* takes place: a vast living world shaped by countless peoples, ancient conflicts, shifting borders, and the ever-present influence of Aethra.

Its known lands are inhabited by Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Dragonkind, Hiveborn, Goblins, Demonkind, and many other peoples. Across Terra, kingdoms rise beside ancient forests, civilizations flourish beneath mountains, free cities connect distant cultures, and territories beyond established borders remain only partially explored.

Although its peoples differ greatly in appearance, culture, history, and their relationship with Aethra, no race in Terra is inherently aligned with good or evil. Nations and peoples form alliances, wage wars, trade, migrate, divide, and reunite according to their own histories and choices.

The largest known landmass in the current setting is commonly referred to as the **Great Continent**, where most of the documented civilizations and political powers of the present era are located.

## The Great Continent

The Great Continent contains a wide range of environments and civilizations.

Human kingdoms occupy much of its central and northern regions. The ancient Elven realm of Sylvaris lies within the Great Forest, while the Dwarven Holds extend through subterranean territories. Dragonkind controls Great Dragon Mountain, which few outsiders enter freely.

Farther south lie territories associated with the Demon Domains, each shaped by different peoples, histories, and political traditions.

The Free City of Arklune stands near the southern frontier of Eldoria and serves as an important meeting point for travelers and Adventurers from many backgrounds.

The known political map of Terra has never been permanent. Borders have moved repeatedly through war, migration, diplomacy, and the rise and fall of regional powers.

## Peoples of Terra

Terra is home to many intelligent peoples.

Among the best documented are Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Dragonkind, Hiveborn, Goblins, and the many distinct peoples historically or politically grouped under Demonkind.

The terms **race**, **Demonkind**, and **monster** are not interchangeable.

Many creatures commonly called monsters are non-sapient or highly predatory forms of Aethra-influenced wildlife. Other peoples have sometimes been labeled “monsters” by neighboring societies despite possessing language, families, traditions, and communities of their own.

## Major Civilizations

The present era recognizes several major powers whose influence extends far beyond their immediate borders.

These include the Human alliance known as the [**Grand Concord**]({{ '/wiki/factions/grand-concord/' | relative_url }}), the Elven civilization of the [**Wardens of the Great Tree**]({{ '/wiki/locations/sylvaris/' | relative_url }}), the [**Dwarven Holds**]({{ '/wiki/factions/dwarven-holds/' | relative_url }}), [**The Hive**]({{ '/wiki/factions/the-hive/' | relative_url }}), [**The Dragon Throne**]({{ '/wiki/factions/dragon-throne/' | relative_url }}), and the southern political order commonly associated with the [**Seven Sigils**]({{ '/wiki/factions/seven-sigils/' | relative_url }}).

These powers do not represent every people on Terra. Independent cities, tribes, settlements, frontier communities, and smaller states exist throughout the continent.

Relations between the major powers vary from trade and diplomacy to rivalry and open conflict.

## Geography

The **Great Forest** is one of the continent's largest ancient ecosystems and is closely associated with the Elven realm.

**Great Dragon Mountain** is another major landmark and the heartland of Dragonkind. Its summit holds the sacred high seat known as the Dragon Throne.

The central and northern regions contain the principal Human kingdoms, while the southern territories include multiple Demon Domains.

Between established states lie frontier regions, wilderness, old roads, abandoned settlements, monster habitats, and ruins whose origins are not always known.

As a result, even modern maps of Terra remain incomplete.

## Aethra and the Living World

Aethra is present throughout Terra.

It exists within living beings and throughout the natural environment, making magic possible and influencing the development of many forms of life.

Some peoples use Aethra through learned disciplines such as magic. Others possess biological or cultural relationships with it that have developed over generations.

In some species, Aethra is also associated with **Natural Evolution**, through which an individual may develop into a more advanced or specialized form of its existing lineage.

Aethra itself is not regarded as inherently benevolent or malevolent. It can be used to heal, create, protect, destroy, or conquer.

Its true origin remains unknown.

## Known History

One of the most significant conflicts in recent Human history was the war against the forces of Beta, culminating in the **Battle of Asteron** approximately forty-three years before the present era.

The defenders succeeded in stopping the invasion, but the battle devastated Fort Asteron and the surrounding region.

The conflict also exposed the inability of the Human kingdoms to coordinate quickly against a large external invasion. In its aftermath, five Human crowns established the **Grand Concord** to improve cooperation against future threats.

Asteron itself was eventually abandoned after an unexplained disruption in the local flow of Aethra made permanent habitation increasingly difficult. The phenomenon became known as **Aethra Distortion**, and the affected region later became known as the **Aethra Scar**.

Terra's history, however, extends far beyond surviving written records.

Ruins, forgotten roads, lost settlements, and artifacts continue to suggest that much of the world's past remains unknown.
