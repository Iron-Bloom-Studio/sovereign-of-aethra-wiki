# Wiki Style Guide

Article titles use Title Case. The first paragraph is a short, neutral definition of the subject.

Write as an encyclopedia: state released facts plainly; avoid novel prose, marketing language, speculation, and developer notes.

Character pages describe known facts. Race pages never generalize a single character's traits to an entire people. Faction pages separate political identity from biological race. Evolution pages distinguish Race, Natural Evolution, Class, and Authority.

Omit an unknown field or section. Never expose internal editorial labels or unreleased-material notes to readers.
