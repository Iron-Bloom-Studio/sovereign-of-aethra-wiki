# Public Race Publication Rules

**Scope:** Internal editorial guidance for the public SOA Wiki  
**Reader-facing article:** No

1. Public Wiki follows **revealed canon**, not all private canon.
2. Race pages describe biological identity and publicly revealed Natural Evolution.
3. Hero and Demon Lord are Authority/title concepts, not races.
4. Do not create Humanity / Demonkind / Neutral biological branches.
5. Do not expose private evolution branches before narrative reveal.
6. Luca remains **Human** until an approved manuscript changes reader knowledge.
7. Do not expose hidden Luca race or Authority information.
8. Social terms such as Demonkind or Monster may appear where canonically used, but must not silently become biological taxonomy.
9. Missing information stays missing.
10. Uncertain publication decisions become **AUTHOR REVIEW REQUIRED**.
