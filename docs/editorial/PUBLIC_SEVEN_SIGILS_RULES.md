# Seven Demon Sigils — Public Publication Rules

**Purpose:** Internal editorial guidance. This is not a reader-facing lore article.

1. Exactly seven Demon Sigils are known: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Theta, Sigma, and Omega.
2. Demon Sigil means Ancient Authority; Demon Lord means recognized bearer/title.
3. Demon Lord is not a race, Natural Evolution, Class, faction, civilization, or political state.
4. Dominion is the shared principle; Sigil-specific criteria remain unrevealed until approved.
5. Do not biologically restrict Demon Sigils to Demonkind or permanently associate a particular Sigil with a race.
6. Political inheritance and Authority inheritance are separate; Demon Lord Authority is not automatically hereditary.
7. Keep Demon Sigil, recognized bearer, and political domain distinct. A domain may continue without an active bearer.
8. Do not invent origins, creators, post-death mechanics, evolution mechanics, hidden eligibility, or Recognition criteria.
9. Public Wiki follows revealed canon only; private hypotheses and future concepts must not leak publicly.
10. Unknown origin and intentionally unanswered rules are not documentation errors.
11. New revelations require explicit author and publication review.
